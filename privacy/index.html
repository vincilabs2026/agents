Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Vinci Labs handles information collected through ai.vincilabs.us, our demo and strategy-call bookings, and our Facebook and Instagram lead forms. It covers these marketing and inquiry activities, rather than the handling of customer data by a separately contracted AI receptionist service.

Information collected

When you contact us, submit a lead form or book a call, we collect the information you provide. This can include your name, email address, telephone number, business name, business role, appointment details and answers about your business needs. Please do not include sensitive customer records, health information or payment details in an inquiry.

The website and embedded services can also receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referring links and interactions with the site.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to respond to your request, understand your business requirements, assess whether our services may be suitable, arrange and manage calls, and follow up about the inquiry. We use website and advertising event information to understand campaign performance and improve our website and advertising. You can ask us to stop inquiry-related promotional follow-up by emailing the address below.

Services involved

  • Meta: Facebook and Instagram process information when you use their lead forms or interact with our ads. The website uses the Meta Pixel to report page visits, booking-button clicks, demo-play requests, active-time milestones and completed appointment bookings for advertising measurement and attribution. Meta may collect or receive browser, device, cookie and event information and use it for measurement and advertising under its own policies. See Meta's Privacy Policy and ad preferences.
  • Calendly: Our embedded calendar processes the contact and appointment information you submit to arrange a call. See Calendly's Privacy Notice.
  • Google: Embedded demo videos and video preview images are served through Google Drive. Loading or playing this content can send technical information to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Website hosting and business communications: Hosting, email and connected service providers process information needed to deliver the website, receive inquiries and communicate with you.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy notices describe their processing and available choices.

Cookies and browser storage

The website and embedded providers use cookies, pixels or browser storage for functionality and measurement. The booking page uses session storage to display booking details on the confirmation page and reduce duplicate booking events. You can manage browser storage and cookies in your browser, use available embedded-provider cookie controls, and manage Meta advertising preferences through your Meta account. Blocking these technologies may affect booking displays, embedded content or measurement.

Retention

Retention depends on the purpose of the information: responding to your inquiry, managing an appointment, continuing a business discussion or relationship, and meeting applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may contact us to request deletion when information is no longer needed. Third-party providers apply their own retention practices to information they control.

Your requests and choices

To ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or stop follow-up, email vincilabs26@gmail.com. Include enough context for us to identify your inquiry. We may need to verify that a request relates to your information. Requests involving information controlled independently by Meta, Calendly or Google may also need to be addressed to that provider.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when these activities change. The update date appears above. For privacy questions, contact Vinci Labs at vincilabs26@gmail.com.
